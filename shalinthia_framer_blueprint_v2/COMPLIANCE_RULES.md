# Compliance Rules — Source of Truth

## Program Framing

Correct:
- Brooklyn 60-Day Seller Success Blueprint
- structured 60-day marketing and coordination program
- eligible properties
- qualified homeowners
- see if your property qualifies
- qualification review
- no-pressure qualification review

Incorrect:
- CLOSED IN 60™
- Closed in 60 Days
- 60-Day Guarantee
- guaranteed sale
- guaranteed closing
- guaranteed price
- guaranteed profit

## Mandatory Disclaimers

Program:
> The Brooklyn 60-Day Seller Success Blueprint is a structured marketing and coordination program for eligible properties.

No guarantee:
> No agent can guarantee sale price, profit, buyer behavior, lender timelines, appraisal outcomes, title clearance, or closing date.

AI:
> AI tools are used as assistive aids in content creation and market analysis. All content is reviewed and finalized by a licensed real estate professional.

## Client-Centered Standard

Every recommendation must be framed as:
- guided by the client's wishes
- grounded in careful analysis
- informed by local expertise
- focused on protecting client equity

## Fair Housing / Advertising

- Do not use discriminatory or demographic-targeting language.
- Do not imply protected-class preferences.
- Do not make unsubstantiated “best,” “top,” or similar superiority claims.
- Do not misstate license type.
- Include brokerage identification and required contact information in public-facing advertising.
- All final public-facing materials should be reviewed for brokerage compliance before publication.
