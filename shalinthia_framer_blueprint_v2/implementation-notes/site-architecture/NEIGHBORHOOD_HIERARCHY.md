## Nested authority routes

- Flatbush: Ditmas Park, Prospect Lefferts Gardens, Wingate. Additional coverage: Flatbush Village Historic Core, Beverley Squares, Beverley Square East/West, Fiske Terrace.
- East Flatbush: Remsen Village, Wingate, Erasmus. Additional coverage: Rugby, Farragut / Hyde Park, Ditmas Village, Northeast East Flatbush.
- East New York: New Lots, Spring Creek, Broadway Junction. Additional coverage: City Line, Cypress Hills, Starrett City / Spring Creek Towers, Highland Park, The Hole.
- Brownsville: Betsy Head. Additional coverage: Ocean Hill / Ocean Hill-Brownsville. Prioritize community identity, homeowner stories, property ownership, and boundaries over excessive micro-pages.
- Long Island City: Hunters Point, Court Square. Additional coverage: Dutch Kills, Queensbridge, Ravenswood, Blissville, Queensboro Plaza, Queens Plaza, Gantry Plaza / Queens West Waterfront.

## Required neighborhood-page structure

1. Neighborhood-specific homeowner hero and relevant photorealistic local/property visual.
2. Local expertise: characteristics, homeowner concerns, property patterns, and buyer demand.
3. Property types.
4. Real-Time Market Insights, linked to `/market-insights`.
5. Homeowner FAQ/AEO.
6. Video module, branded neighborhood guide, and private-consultation CTA.

## Linking

Each page links to Market Insights, Seller Services, Downsizing, Private Consultation, and relevant guides. The Brooklyn 60-Day Seller Success Blueprint is presented only when appropriate as a signature program, never as the site identity.
