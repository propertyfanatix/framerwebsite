# Shalinthia Miles Website Voice & Messaging Rules

## Purpose

This document defines the public-facing communication standards for the Shalinthia Miles brand.

All website copy, Canva assets, social media content, blog posts, neighborhood pages, lead magnets, videos, and AI-generated drafts must follow these rules.

The goal is to communicate complex strategy in language that feels natural, familiar, and trustworthy to homeowners.

---

# Brand Voice

Primary personality:

- Trusted advisor
- Friendly neighbor
- Experienced professional
- Community-focused advocate

The audience should feel:

- Heard
- Respected
- Educated
- Confident
- Supported

Avoid language that feels:

- Corporate
- Sales-driven
- Pressuring
- Overly technical
- Like industry jargon

---

# Internal Strategy vs Public Language

Some strategic concepts guide our approach internally but should NOT appear in consumer-facing materials.

## Prohibited Public Terms

Never use:

- Tactical Empathy
- Ethical Urgency

These are internal strategy concepts only.

---

# Translation Rules

## Internal Concept:

Tactical Empathy

## Never write:

"Using tactical empathy..."

"Applying tactical empathy..."

## Instead write:

- Understanding what matters most to you
- Listening before recommending a solution
- Creating a strategy based on your goals
- Taking time to understand your situation
- Providing guidance that respects your decisions
- Helping homeowners make informed choices

---

## Internal Concept:

Ethical Urgency

## Never write:

"Creating ethical urgency..."

"Using ethical urgency..."

## Instead write:

- Helping you understand your options early
- Creating a clear plan without pressure
- Knowing when the timing is right
- Preparing before decisions become stressful
- Making thoughtful decisions based on the market
- Moving forward with confidence

---

# Consumer Language Standards

Always prioritize:

## Familiar Words

Use:

- Plan
- Strategy
- Options
- Guidance
- Confidence
- Preparation
- Understanding
- Protect
- Equity
- Goals
- Timeline
- Support

Avoid:

- Framework
- Methodology
- Tactical
- Optimization
- Leverage
- Conversion
- Funnel
- Disruption
- Algorithmic
- Pain point

---

# Seller Messaging Rules

Never imply:

- Guaranteed sale
- Guaranteed price
- Guaranteed timeline
- Guaranteed buyer behavior

Avoid:

"Sell your home in 60 days guaranteed"

Use:

"Brooklyn 60-Day Seller Success Blueprint"

Description:

"A structured 60-day marketing and coordination program designed to help eligible properties move forward with a clear strategy."

---

# Emotional Positioning

The homeowner is not a lead.

The homeowner is:

- A neighbor
- A family member
- A longtime community member
- Someone making an important life decision

Write with respect.

---

# Senior Homeowner Communication

For longtime homeowners:

Do:

- Acknowledge memories
- Respect decades of ownership
- Recognize emotional decisions
- Explain options clearly

Do not:

- Create fear
- Shame aging homes
- Suggest they failed to maintain property
- Pressure downsizing

---

# AI Content Rules

AI may assist with:

- Research
- Organization
- Drafting
- Market explanations
- Visual concepts

AI must not replace:

- Professional judgment
- Real estate expertise
- Client conversations

Public language:

"AI-assisted marketing reviewed by a licensed real estate professional."

---

# Website Content Test

Before publishing, ask:

1. Would a Brooklyn homeowner naturally understand this?
2. Does this sound like advice from a trusted neighbor?
3. Does this respect the homeowner's choices?
4. Does this explain value without pressure?
5. Does this build confidence?

If not, rewrite.

---

# Preferred Brand Statement

Use variations of:

"I help Brooklyn homeowners make confident real estate decisions through local expertise, thoughtful strategy, and personalized guidance."

---

# Final Rule

Strategy can be sophisticated.

The language should feel simple.

The homeowner should understand the value without needing to understand the strategy behind it.
