## Brand Hierarchy

Shalinthia Miles is the primary brand.

The approved broader positioning is:

"I help clients make intelligent real estate decisions every day." (her positioning, 2026-10-01; she is "not only focused on Brooklyn")

My Promise is the governing client-service philosophy.

The Brooklyn 60-Day Seller Success Blueprint is one signature seller program. It is not the entire business or sole offering.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Preserve the existing Framer visual identity.
- Do not redesign the brand.
- Do not make the Brooklyn 60-Day Seller Success Blueprint the identity of the entire website.
- Do not force every visitor into Blueprint qualification.
- Use the page's visitor intent to select the CTA.
- Do not use CLOSED IN 60.
- Do not use CLOSED IN 60™.
- Do not use Closed in 60 Days.
- Do not use guarantee language.
- Do not make AI the main brand.
- Do not make eXp Realty the main brand.
- eXp requires co-branding on all marketing: use the Shalinthia | eXp Realty co-branded logo (brand/logos/). It goes in the footer of every page and on every marketing visual. The signature or eXp logo alone does not count.
- Do not add a "Brokered by" line. It is not required in the USA.
- Official brand assets live in Google Drive › Brand Assets Library. Never generate, redraw or recolor a logo.
- Gold (#B08D57) is retired. Follow Shalinthia's guidelines v2026.2 (see BRAND_2026.md).
- Use "My Promise," never "The Shalinthia Promise."

BRAND_2026.md controls over every other file in this repo. Among the older files, implementation-notes/BRAND_HIERARCHY.md controls the brand hierarchy. Shalinthia's explicit instructions override both.
