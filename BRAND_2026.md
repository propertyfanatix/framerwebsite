# Brand and co-branding rules: current (2026-09-29)

**This file controls.** Older brand, compliance and footer notes in this repo are out of date. Where any file here conflicts with this one, this file wins unless Shalinthia says otherwise. It mirrors the SOS brand standard (`propertyfanatix/SOS`: `config/brand.yaml`, `docs/BRAND.md`).

## Shalinthia's standing instructions

1. **eXp requires co-branding on all marketing.**
2. **"My branding dictates and use the cobranded logo."** Co-branding means the Shalinthia | eXp Realty **co-branded logo**. The signature or the eXp logo on its own, or the two placed side by side, does not count.
3. **No "Brokered by" credit line.** It is not required in the USA. Do not add it to the footer or anywhere else, and do not flag its absence.
4. **Texts are signed, not co-branded.** SMS and Smart Plan texts name Shalinthia and may attach her signature as a sign-off image. They never carry the co-branded logo.
5. **Official brand assets live in Google Drive › Brand Assets Library.** Use those files. Never generate, redraw, retype or recolor a logo.

**On the website:**
- Every page carries the co-branded logo in the footer (the reusable Footer component).
- The Navigation keeps the existing Shalinthia signature logo on the left.
- On landing pages, the CTA wins above the fold, but the co-branded logo still appears in the header or footer.

**Every other marketing piece with a visual** carries the co-branded logo. This covers ads, lead forms, emails, social posts, print, video and lead magnets.

Google Search ads are text-only; their co-branding is the landing page they point to.

**Logo rules:**
- Scale proportionally.
- Keep clear space on every side of at least the height of the capital S.
- No stretching, rotation, recoloring, shadows, outlines or glows.
- Never place a logo over a busy photo without a solid panel behind it.

## Brand system (Shalinthia's guidelines v2026.2)

- **Name treatment:** the signature logo image only; never retyped in any font. Black is the default.
- **Colors:**

  | Name | Value | Role |
  |---|---|---|
  | Burgundy (Framer: Oxblood) | `#5E0000` | Primary accent |
  | Navy | `#0D1028` | Secondary |
  | Charcoal (Framer: Ink) | `#24221F` | All body text |
  | Cream (Framer: Ivory) | `#F6F3EE` / `#F4F1EA` | Backgrounds |
  | Beige | `#D9D3CB` | Dividers, frames, gridlines |

  **Gold `#B08D57` is retired everywhere.**
- **Themes by material.** The co-branded logo goes on all of them.
  - **Landing pages:** cream-forward, with burgundy and navy as thin accents only (buttons, rules, labels). No full-width color panels, not photo-heavy, no gold.
  - **One-pagers:** navy panel for the photo/CTA block, cream base.
  - **Lead magnets:** burgundy cover, cream interior.
  - **eXp co-branded pieces:** white or cream, with burgundy as the sole accent.
  - **Social:** cream, with a local photo.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond for titles, headings and quotes (never below 18pt). Inter for body, labels, buttons and data. No third typeface. Use Large Print sizes for longtime-homeowner materials.
- **Imagery:**
  - Hyperlocal, real neighborhood photos.
  - AI images labeled "AI-generated digital mockup".
  - One approved headshot, used consistently.
- **Hierarchy (unchanged):** Shalinthia Miles is the primary brand, followed by private advisory and market intelligence, then My Promise, then programs. The Blueprint is a program, never a guarantee. Never use "CLOSED IN 60" or guarantee language.
